A consumer satellite-monitoring service returns prose, not masks: a subscriber
pins a neighbourhood and expects to be told what changed after each revisit. We
build that service from the current AI stack --- a promptable segmentation
foundation model (\DetectorName) for open-vocabulary instance detection, CLIP
embeddings for appearance, a persistent spatio-temporal knowledge graph for
history, and a large language model (\LLMName) for the report --- and we
evaluate it on what it writes rather than only on what it segments. Two failures
that segmentation metrics cannot see become product failures: a report that
invents construction, and a report that miscounts it. We find that neither is
fixed where practitioners usually reach. Adding retrieval or graph aggregation
to the language model's context leaves factuality essentially unchanged; what
moves it is the quality of the upstream instance evidence, and specifically the
step of deciding which detections across two revisits are the same physical
object. Every pairing strategy we tested that does not learn --- geometry, a
hand-tuned CLIP-plus-geometry heuristic, and the strongest published zero-shot
method --- lands in the same narrow band regardless of segmentation quality. A
\HeadParamsRound-parameter pairing head, supervised for free from
change-detection masks, leaves it, reaching \EOnePixelFOne{} pixel F1 on
LEVIR-CD against \AnyChangeFOne{} for the zero-shot peer; applied unchanged to
WHU-CD --- a domain it has never seen, no retraining, no threshold tuning --- it
reaches \ETwoPixelFOne{} against \ETwoHeuristicFOne{} for the heuristic it
replaces, cutting instance count error from \ETwoHeuristicCountMAE{} to
\ETwoCountMAE. To measure reports we introduce Change-Fact-Score, which reduces
a report to atomic direction--class--count claims with a
deterministic extractor and scores them against human references and
ground-truth masks, so no language model sits inside the metric. It exposes what
n-gram overlap hides: a capable vision--language model shown both images writes
fluent prose while deciding at chance (\EFourVLMChgAcc\%) whether anything
changed at all. Holding the language model and its grounding fixed and swapping
only the pairing moves report factuality by \EFiveGain{} points, at a cost of
\ESixHeadDelta~ms per tile --- \ESixPipelineShare\% of a pipeline dominated by
segmentation.
