\section{Related Work}

\textbf{Supervised change detection.} Remote-sensing change detection has been
studied extensively using Siamese CNNs and transformer-based architectures.
Representative methods include STANet~\cite{chen2020spatial},
SNUNet~\cite{fang2021snunet}, BIT~\cite{chen2021remote},
ChangeFormer~\cite{bandara2022transformer}, and
TinyCD~\cite{codegoni2023tinycd}, which report 86--91 F1 on LEVIR-CD when
trained on its designated training split and predict a binary change mask.
Neither property suits a
service that must name and count objects in a neighborhood it was never
trained on, so we treat them as a reference ceiling rather than as competitors.
AnyChange~\cite{zheng2024segment} instead adapts SAM to change detection
without training on the target dataset, reaching \AnyChangeFOne{} F1 on
LEVIR-CD and \AnyChangeHFOne{} in a human-assisted three-point setting. That is
our league. Our own position is a third one we state rather than blur: the
detector has never seen LEVIR-CD, but the pairing head is trained on its
training split from mask-derived labels, so the LEVIR-CD number is not
zero-shot. The WHU-CD experiment, where the same head is applied without
retraining, carries the zero-shot claim.

\textbf{Change captioning and grounding.} RSICC-style
models~\cite{hoxha2022change,liu2022remote,liu2023progressive,chang2023changes}
generate a sentence per image pair, scored with BLEU, METEOR, ROUGE-L and
CIDEr-D against human references. They are trained on the caption corpus, and
n-gram overlap rewards phrasing that matches the reference distribution. Our
concern is orthogonal: a report can score well on overlap while asserting a
change that did not occur. Retrieval-augmented
generation~\cite{lewis2020retrieval} reduces unsupported assertions by
conditioning on retrieved evidence, and graph-based
variants~\cite{edge2024local} aggregate over entities rather than top-$k$
passages. Evaluating the result is the harder half, and metrics that use a
language model as judge~\cite{zheng2023judging} inherit its failure modes and
can be gamed by the model under test. We take the opposite
approach in Section~\ref{ssec:cfs}: nothing in the metric is learned.
